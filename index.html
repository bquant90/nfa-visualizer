Load a machine to see its drawing.

Circles are states. Arrows are rules, labeled with the characters they read. The arrow coming from nowhere points at the start state. A double circle is an accepting (winning) state. While a string runs, yellow circles are where the machine could be right now, orange arrows were just used, and a red dashed circle is a guess that hit a dead end. At the very end, green means the string was accepted and red means it wasn't.
